# tortoise-mico-mobile-home
A Low-Cost , High Quality, Micro-Home Designed to be easily made for induvials experiencing homelessness

Mobile Micro Homes Concept:

This project was born from a simple fact: Many people are one step away from homelessness.

Every person experiencing homelessness deserves dignity, safety, and a path toward stability.

The mobile micro-home concept aims to provide immediate, an affordable livable micro home for currently un-sheltered individuals in communities. 

Unlike traditional shelter systems that require people to navigate complex bureaucracies or leave their belongings and pets behind, these units offer autonomous, portable homes that preserves dignity, provides a way to keep their pets and belongings while protecting them from the elements.

How this idea came into being and why it’s needed:

I have always had an interest in affordable practical innovative housing, I'm trying to create the absolute most affordable, accessible, maintainable home that's possible to create, with the goal of helping un-housed people who are having to sleep in tents, and on the ground in all types of weather, having to feel insecure, having to worry and carry their possessions in bags.

I have been working over several months on an idea that could help, a practical affordable easy to build and maintain mobile micro-home.

I’ve had several friends that have experienced homelessness over their life times.  I’ve seen people sleeping on sidewalks, in doorways, and on the ground in parking lots.  Walking, alone, carrying their only worldly possessions in bags.
